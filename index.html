The B-Day Method

The B-Day Method is a simple training approach for seeing calendar patterns and finding weekdays mentally. It is inspired by the classic Doomsday rule developed by John Horton Conway.

July 4 and October 31
always land on the same weekday in the same year.

04/04 · 06/06 · 08/08 · 10/10 · 12/12
same pattern. Same system.

Every 400 years, weekdays repeat.
June 6, 744 has the same weekday as June 6, 1944.

Background: Doomsday rule · John Horton Conway
